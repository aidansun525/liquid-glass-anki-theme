## What's new in 1.1

- **No more flashing:** on some PCs the glass card flashed every time a new card appeared. The card now stays on screen and only its text changes.
- **Card height now stays put:** the card keeps exactly the height you set in Settings on every card. Long cards scroll inside the card instead of stretching it. Very tall settings are limited only by the size of your window.
- Prefer the old behavior? Slide **Card height** all the way left (*Fit to card*) and the card sizes itself to each card.
