**A calm, good-looking Anki.** Your cards sit on frosted glass over a wallpaper that runs behind every screen, and each answer gets a soft glow and a quiet piano note.

![Soft glow after answering Good](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aidansun525/liquid-glass-anki-theme/main/docs/screenshots/glow_good.jpg)

## Features

- **Wallpaper behind every screen**: deck list, overview, reviewer, and the top and bottom bars, lined up seamlessly in light and dark mode.
- **New wallpapers every day**: professional photos (Nature, Architecture, Landscape, or your own tags) or anime art, safe-rated only, from Wallhaven and Konachan. You can also turn downloads off and keep one picture. Four built-in film photographs are included, and you can add your own.
- **Frosted-glass card** with adjustable opacity, blur, width, height and corners. Cards keep a steady size.
- **Answer feedback**: a soft glow and a short piano note, different for Again, Hard, Good and Easy. You can adjust the volume or turn either off.
- **Card text size** slider. It changes only the size, so bold, colors and links stay as they are.
- **New wallpaper** button on the deck list, and Ctrl+Shift+W anywhere.
- One **Settings** window (Tools → Frosted Glass → Settings). Changes show up live.

## Good to know

> ⚠️ **ALL DOWNLOADED PICTURES COME FROM THE INTERNET AND MAY BE INAPPROPRIATE FOR PROFESSIONAL SETTINGS. FOR THE SAFEST OPTION, TURN OFF RANDOM WALLPAPERS** (set *New pictures per day* to *Off* and pick a built-in picture). Only pictures rated "safe" by their uploaders are downloaded.

- Want a wallpaper that never changes? Click it on the Wallpaper tab and set *New pictures per day* to *Off*.
- If you use *Custom Background Image and Gear Icon*, your pictures and settings are imported and that add-on is turned off, so the backgrounds don't overlap.
- Pictures you add yourself are never deleted. Only downloaded ones are cleaned up after a few days.
