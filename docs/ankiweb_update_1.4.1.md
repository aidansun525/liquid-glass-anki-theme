## What's new in 1.4.1

- **Crash fix for Mac:** Anki could quit unexpectedly when headphones or a speaker disconnected, for example AirPods switching away. The piano notes now play through Anki's own audio player, so this can't happen any more.
- **Speaker choice:** if you pick a speaker in *Play on*, the note starts about 0.2 seconds after you answer. Choose *System default* for an instant note.

## 1.4

- **Move the card up or down:** Settings → Glass & sound → *Distance from top*.
- **Deck-done chime:** a soft piano chime, in the same tone as the answer notes, when you finish a deck. It doesn't play if you leave early, and it follows the Piano note switch, volume and speaker choice.

## 1.3

- **Choose your speakers:** pick which speakers or headphones play the piano note in Settings → Glass & sound → *Play on*. If the one you picked isn't connected, the system default is used.

## 1.2

- **Less flashing on new cards:** on some Windows PCs the card and glass panel still flashed when a new card loaded. The glass is now a separate layer behind the card, so loading a card never redraws it, and the new text simply replaces the old.

## 1.1

- **No more flashing:** on some PCs the glass card flashed every time a new card appeared. The card now stays on screen and only its text changes.
- **Card height fixed:** in smaller Anki windows the card couldn't reach the height you set. Your card height is now the minimum on every card, up to the full height of the window, and longer cards still grow past it.
- **Your settings carry over:** updating keeps your settings and pictures. If you installed from a file before and now use the AnkiWeb code (or the other way around), the new copy brings over your settings and pictures, turns the old copy off, and shows you a short notice. Restart Anki once afterwards.
