Frosted Glass: Anki Aesthetic Overhaul
======================================
https://github.com/aidansun525/liquid-glass-anki-theme

  * a wallpaper behind every Anki screen, lined up across the top bar,
    main screen and bottom bar (background.py)
  * a few new wallpapers a day: professional photos (Wallhaven General),
    or anime art (Konachan / Wallhaven), safe-rated only (wallpaper_fetcher/)
  * a frosted-glass review card with a soft glow and piano note when you
    answer (frosted_glass/)

Everything is under Tools > Frosted Glass > Settings. Ctrl+Shift+W shows a
new wallpaper.

Your files and settings live in user_files/ (kept across updates):
  background/            wallpapers (add your own here)
  gear/                  deck gear icons
  css/custom_*.css       optional extra CSS per screen
  glass_sounds/          optional again/hard/good/easy .wav files of your own
  wallpaper_fetcher.json, frosted_glass.json   settings
